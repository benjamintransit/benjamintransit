// Questions
FAQ
Is the configurator really free?
Yes, completely. Design your panel, download the STL, print it yourself — no payment, no account, no catch.
What are the STL shop files for?
Pre-designed panels ready to slice and print. If you want full control over slots and labels, use the free configurator instead.
How do I get my file after buying?
Payhip delivers the STL to your email instantly after checkout. No waiting, no account required to download.
What slicer settings should I use?
0.2mm layer height, 3 walls, 20% infill works well for most panels. A full print guide is included with each paid file.
What size are the button holes?
Standard 16mm momentary buttons and 12mm toggle switches. All holes sized to fit common off-the-shelf hardware.
Can I customise a paid STL?
Paid files are fixed STLs. For a fully custom layout and labels, use the free configurator — it's built exactly for that.
